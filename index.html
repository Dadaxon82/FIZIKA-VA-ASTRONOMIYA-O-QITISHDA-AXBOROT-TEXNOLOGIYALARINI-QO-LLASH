Parametrlar & Boshqaruv

Eyler-Kromer
Differensial tenglama:
$$\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2} + \gamma \frac{d\theta}{dt} + \frac{g}{L}\sin\theta = 0$$
Mayatnik uzinligi ($L$): 1.50 m
Boshlang'ich burchak ($\theta_0$): 60.0°
So'nish koeffitsienti ($\gamma$): 0.15 s⁻¹
Gravitatsiya muhiti ($g$): 9.81 m/s² (Yer)
Dinamik mayatnik animatsiyasi t: 0.00s | E: 100%
Faza fazosi trayektoriyasi ($\theta$ vs $\omega$) Attraktor spirali
Fizika dvigateli JavaScript kodi

Lorents parametrlari

F = q(E + v×B)
Larmor radiusi & siklotron chastotasi:
$$R_L = \frac{m v_\perp}{q B}, \quad \omega_c = \frac{q B}{m}$$
Magnit maydon induktsiyasi ($B$): 1.5 T
Zarracha zaryadi ($q$): +1.0 e
Ko'ndalang tezlik ($v_\perp$): 2.0 m/s
Bo'ylama tezlik ($v_\parallel$): 0.8 m/s
Zaryadlangan zarraning spiral harakati RL: 1.33m | wc: 1.50 rad/s

Doppler parametrlari

Akustika & Optika
Doppler chastota siljishi:
$$f' = f_0 \left(\frac{v_{tolqin}}{v_{tolqin} \mp v_{manba}}\right)$$
Manba tezligi (Mach soni): 0.60 M
To'lqin chiqarish davri: 12 kadr
Harakatlanuvchi manbadan chiqayotgan to'lqin frontlari Tovushdan past (Subsonik)

Yung tajribasi

$$d \sin\theta = m \lambda, \quad \Delta x = \frac{\lambda D}{d}$$

Ekran masofasi ($D$): 1.20 m

2.55 mm
